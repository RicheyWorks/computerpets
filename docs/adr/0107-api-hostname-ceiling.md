# 0107. HPA ceiling matches the six healthy API hostnames

- **Status:** Accepted (the one Ready zone ceiling is [0108](0108-api-single-zone-hostname-ceiling.md); the healthy-pool ceiling of 6 is the part this ADR set and 0108 replaces)
- **Date:** 2026-09-23
- **Code:** `deploy/k8s/hpa.yaml`; `deploy/terraform/modules/node_pool/main.tf`; `deploy/k8s/check-api-hostname-ceiling.sh`

## Context

[0106](0106-api-single-zone-hostname-floor.md) left this gap: above the floor, ten pods on six healthy hostnames still share nodes (2, 2, 2, 2, 1, and 1). On one Ready zone that ceiling is 4, 3, and 3. Inventory on `main` tip `c4f29f016`:

| Surface | What it did | What it did not do |
|---------|-------------|--------------------|
| `deploy/k8s/hpa.yaml` | `minReplicas: 3`, `maxReplicas: 10`, target `computerpets-blue`. Not in the kustomization | Not a hostname count. The ceiling is pods |
| `min_size_per_zone` | A local, `3`. Create-time `desired_size` is `3`, ignored after create. Two zones are six hostnames | Not ten hostnames. Not five per zone |
| Hostname item ([0100](0100-api-hostname-hard-spread.md)) | `kubernetes.io/hostname`, `maxSkew: 1`, `DoNotSchedule`, Honor policies, `minDomains` unset, no required anti-affinity | On six hostnames, 10 pods is 2, 2, 2, 2, 1, and 1. That skew is legal. The extra pods bind. Nothing stays Pending, so the leader does not add a node |
| Zone item ([0095](0095-api-zone-hard-spread.md)) | `topology.kubernetes.io/zone`, `maxSkew: 1`, `DoNotSchedule`, `minDomains` unset | One eligible zone still takes every ceiling pod. The zone item does not invent a second zone |
| `max_size_per_zone` | `20` ([0104](0104-per-zone-node-max.md)), sized as twice the old ceiling of 10 | A growth cap. Not a reason to keep asking for 10 pods |
| Kind and minikube | Blue `replicas: 2` on one hostname. `enable_node_pool=false` does not plan the groups. `hpa.yaml` is not in the kustomization | Must still schedule. `minDomains` and required hostname anti-affinity would leave the second local replica Pending |

Why the healthy pool stacks. Hostname `DoNotSchedule` with `maxSkew` 1 allows a difference of one. A second pod on a hostname is illegal only while some other eligible hostname is still empty: the skew would be 2. After every hostname holds one, the next pod's skew is 1, so it binds. Six hostnames absorb six pods as 1 each. Pods 7 through 10 are the second layer. The placement is 2, 2, 2, 2, 1, and 1. Cluster Autoscaler sees no Pending pod.

Why six is the ceiling that does not stack. The same rule, on six hostnames, never reaches a second pod when the HPA stops at 6. Zone `maxSkew` 1 places those six as 3 and 3. Each zone's three hostnames place those three as 1 each. That is `maxReplicas` equal to two zones times `min_size_per_zone`, not a larger bill.

Why not raise `min_size` and keep 10. Five hostnames per zone is ten nodes, so a healthy pool would hold 10 pods as 1 each. That bills four extra on-demand workers (default `t3.medium`) at all times, including while the API sits at the floor of 3. One Ready zone of five hostnames still places 10 pods as 2 each, which is inside `maxSkew` 1. Ten hostnames per zone would hold that one-zone ceiling as 1 each, and it would bill twenty nodes, the whole [0104](0104-per-zone-node-max.md) max of one zone, while idle. House math already refused a fourth hostname the floor does not use. This slice does not buy hostnames so a burst can avoid sharing.

Why not a ceiling under 6. Five pods on six hostnames do not stack, and they leave a hostname the floor already pays for empty at the peak. Three would match one Ready zone and would also collapse the ceiling onto the floor, so the HPA could not scale. Six is the largest ceiling the healthy floor holds at one pod per hostname.

Why the HPA floor stays 3. The budget keeps 2 pods of the live color during a voluntary eviction. `minReplicas` has to be at least `minAvailable + 1`, which is 3. The ceiling of 6 stays above that floor. The metrics, the behavior windows, and the blue target stay.

`minDomains` is the wrong lever. Unset, it behaves as 1, so one hostname still schedules. A floor of 2 treats the missing hostname as an empty domain and leaves the second pod Pending. Kind and minikube have one node. Required hostname anti-affinity does the same. Required zone anti-affinity would refuse the one Ready zone. Neither is set.

This slice does not reopen presence/CSP, Hikari/replica pool sizing, bundle zip, cosign, CDN edge, secrets rotation, VerifyFieldBounds, ClientAddress, rate-limit bucket sizes, the machine/admin HMAC, the nonce store, the download JWT `jti`, the WAF ACL, Redis AUTH, Postgres JDBC SSL, API listener TLS, the HPA metrics, the API PDB `minAvailable`, the API hostname/zone topology spread, the metrics-server zone `DoNotSchedule` item, the node-pool pin or taint, Cluster Autoscaler replica count, required hostname anti-affinity, leader election, the disruption budget, aws-node, vpc-cni, or the kube-proxy reassert hook beyond this cross-link. The [0104](0104-per-zone-node-max.md) max stays 20. The [0106](0106-api-single-zone-hostname-floor.md) floor stays 3. The [0101](0101-cluster-autoscaler-leader-scale.md) flags stay `--balance-similar-node-groups=true`, `--expander=least-waste`, and `--skip-nodes-with-system-pods=false`. Do not set `minDomains`. Required hostname anti-affinity is not the follow-up. Required zone anti-affinity is not the follow-up. Catalog stays 221. No storefront. No DirectX 12 / Vulkan / Solana. No Rui sprites.

## Decision

**`maxReplicas` is 6. That is two zones times `min_size` 3, so a healthy pool holds the ceiling at one pod per hostname under hostname `maxSkew` 1. `minReplicas` stays 3. `min_size` and the create-time `desired_size` stay 3. `max_size` stays 20. Hostname and zone spread stay `DoNotSchedule` with `maxSkew` 1 and `minDomains` unset. Kind and minikube do not apply the HPA and do not plan the pool. No live AWS apply.**

1. **Ceiling.** `maxReplicas` is 6. `hpa-max-replicas=6` and `hostname-ceiling-pods=6` name it. `hostname-floor-nodes=6` is the same count: `min-availability-zones=2` times `min-size-per-zone=3`. The [0078](0078-horizontal-pod-autoscaling.md) ceiling of 10 is refused. A value of 7 stacks as 2, 1, 1, 1, 1, and 1. A value under 6, including 5 and 3, leaves a paid hostname empty or collapses the range onto the floor. There is no variable that overrides the ceiling.
2. **Why 6 does not stack when both zones are up.** Eligible hostnames are the Ready nodes in both groups. Hostname `maxSkew` 1 refuses a second pod while any of those six is empty. Six pods run out at that step. Zone `maxSkew` 1 places them 3 and 3, and each zone's three hostnames place those as 1 each. Similar groups keep the same `min_size`, the same `instance_types`, the same pool label, and the same `NoSchedule` taint. `--balance-similar-node-groups=true` stays.
3. **Why the pool floor does not rise.** `min_size_per_zone` stays 3. `desired_size_per_zone` stays 3. Five per zone would be ten nodes so the old ceiling of 10 could sit 1 each, and one Ready zone would still be 2 each. Ten per zone would be twenty nodes. Both are refused. `max_size_per_zone` stays 20. It is no longer twice this ceiling. A zone at 20 still cannot grow.
4. **Why the HPA floor stays 3.** `minReplicas` stays 3. `minAvailable` stays 2. Three is `minAvailable + 1`. CPU stays 70 percent of the `250m` request. Memory stays an absolute `800Mi`. Scale up stays 2 pods per 60 seconds. Scale down stays a 300 second wait and 1 pod per 60 seconds. The blue target stays.
5. **What this slice does not change.** The hostname item stays `DoNotSchedule`, `maxSkew` 1, Honor, `minDomains` unset. The zone item stays the same shape. Required hostname anti-affinity is not set. Required zone anti-affinity is not set. Do not set minDomains. One hostname still schedules both local blue replicas. The second pod is not left Pending by the hostname item. Terraform still ignores `desired_size` after create (`ignore_changes`). The [0106](0106-api-single-zone-hostname-floor.md) keeper caveat is unchanged: a group whose desired size is still 2, or still 1, needs that bump before `min_size` 3 will apply. The HPA, the PDB, and the autoscaler file stay out of the kustomization. Blue stays `replicas: 2`. Green stays `replicas: 0`. No live AWS apply.
6. **Kind and minikube.** `enable_node_pool=false` does not plan the groups. `kubectl apply -k deploy/k8s` does not apply `hpa.yaml` or `pdb.yaml`. One eligible hostname still runs both local blue replicas once the node carries `computerpets/node-pool=api` and one `topology.kubernetes.io/zone` value. Do not set `minDomains`. Do not add required hostname anti-affinity. Do not taint a kind or minikube node.
7. **Verify without a cluster.** `check-api-hostname-ceiling.sh` fails when `maxReplicas` is not 6 (including the old 10, 7, 5, and 3), when that ceiling is not two zones times `min_size`, when the healthy pool would still stack two ceiling pods on one hostname, when `min_size` is not 3 (including 2, 5, and 10), when `desired_size` is not 3, when `max_size` is not 20, when the zone gate is not two, when hostname `maxSkew` is not 1, when hostname or zone spread leaves `DoNotSchedule` or sets `minDomains`, when required anti-affinity appears, when `minReplicas` is not 3, when `minAvailable` is not 2, when `kustomization.yaml` lists `hpa.yaml`, or when local replica counts move. It also fails closed if one Ready zone would no longer stack this ceiling, because that case is still open. `check-api-hostname-ceiling.test.sh` proves those regressions. No `kubectl apply` and no `terraform apply`.

## Consequences

- The billable floor stays six on-demand workers (default `t3.medium`), three per zone. This slice does not add nodes. Peak replica count drops from 10 to 6. The four pods that used to share nodes are the pods this ceiling will not run. That is the packing cost. The node cost of keeping 10 is four extra workers at the five-per-zone path, or fourteen extra workers at ten per zone, paid while the API is at the floor.
- On a healthy pool the live color's 6 pods are 1 each. A crash of one node takes one of them. Five remain until the HPA or the replacement node catches up. The PDB still keeps 2 during a voluntary drain.
- On one Ready zone the same ceiling is 2, 2, and 2 on three hostnames. Skew is 0, so it is legal. Nothing stays Pending, so the leader does not add hostnames for that ceiling.
- Changing `maxReplicas` does nothing until a keeper applies `hpa.yaml`. This slice does not apply. Terraform still ignores `desired_size` after create.
- Kind and minikube do not plan the pool and do not apply the HPA. One hostname still schedules. The second local replica is not left Pending. Do not set `minDomains`. Required hostname anti-affinity is not set.
- Setting the ceiling back to 10, to 7, or under 6, raising `min_size` to 5 or to 10 to keep the old ceiling, or setting `minDomains` fails `check-api-hostname-ceiling.sh`.
- Catalog stays 221. No Rui sprites. `_*.py` stay untracked.
