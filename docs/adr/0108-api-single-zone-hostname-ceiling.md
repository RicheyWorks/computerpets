# 0108. One Ready zone holds the HPA ceiling

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-09-23
- **Code:** `deploy/k8s/hpa.yaml`; `deploy/terraform/modules/node_pool/main.tf`; `deploy/k8s/check-api-single-zone-hostname-ceiling.sh`

## Context

[0107](0107-api-hostname-ceiling.md) left this gap: on one Ready zone the ceiling of 6 is 2, 2, and 2. Skew is 0, so it binds, and the leader does not add hostnames. Inventory on `main` tip `5ab4f2673`:

| Surface | What it did | What it did not do |
|---------|-------------|--------------------|
| `deploy/k8s/hpa.yaml` | `minReplicas: 3`, `maxReplicas: 6`, target `computerpets-blue`. Not in the kustomization | Six pods. Not three. The ceiling is above the floor |
| `min_size_per_zone` | A local, `3`. Create-time `desired_size` is `3`, ignored after create. Two zones are six hostnames | Not six hostnames in the one zone that is still Ready |
| Hostname item ([0100](0100-api-hostname-hard-spread.md)) | `kubernetes.io/hostname`, `maxSkew: 1`, `DoNotSchedule`, Honor policies, `minDomains` unset, no required anti-affinity | On three hostnames, 6 pods is 2, 2, and 2. That skew is legal. The extra pods bind. Nothing stays Pending |
| Zone item ([0095](0095-api-zone-hard-spread.md)) | `topology.kubernetes.io/zone`, `maxSkew: 1`, `DoNotSchedule`, `minDomains` unset | One eligible zone still takes every ceiling pod |
| `max_size_per_zone` | `20` ([0104](0104-per-zone-node-max.md)) | A growth cap. A zone already at 20 cannot grow. Not a reason to pre-pay that cap |
| Kind and minikube | Blue `replicas: 2` on one hostname. `enable_node_pool=false` does not plan the groups. `hpa.yaml` is not in the kustomization | Must still schedule. `minDomains` and required hostname anti-affinity would leave the second local replica Pending |

Why the one zone stacks. Hostname `DoNotSchedule` with `maxSkew` 1 allows a difference of one. A second pod on a hostname is illegal only while some other eligible hostname is still empty. After every hostname holds one, the next pod's skew is 1, so it binds. Three hostnames absorb three pods as 1 each. Pods 4 through 6 are the second layer. The placement is 2, 2, and 2. Cluster Autoscaler sees no Pending pod.

Why three does not stack. The same rule, on three hostnames, never reaches a second pod when the HPA stops at 3. That is `maxReplicas` equal to `min_size_per_zone`, measured against one Ready zone. It is also equal to `minReplicas`. The range collapses. Scale up and scale down stay in the file and cannot move the count.

Why there is no cheaper middle. A ceiling of N that one zone holds at one pod per hostname needs `min_size` at least N. Similar groups keep the same floor, and the zone gate stays two, so the always-on count is 2N. Nodes per peak pod is 2. That rate does not get cheaper as N grows.

| Ceiling | `min_size` so one zone does not stack | Always-on nodes | Extra nodes beyond the floor bill of 6 | Hostnames per zone the floor of 3 does not use |
|---------|----------------------------------------|-----------------|-----------------------------------------|------------------------------------------------|
| 3 | 3 | 6 | 0 | 0 |
| 4 | 4 | 8 | 2 | 1 |
| 5 | 5 | 10 | 4 | 2 |
| 6 | 6 | 12 | 6 | 3 |
| 10 | 10 | 20 | 14 | 7 |

A ceiling of 4, 5, or 6 while `min_size` stays 3 still stacks (2 and 1 and 1, then 2 and 2 and 1, then 2 and 2 and 2). Those are not closes. Matching `min_size` to 4, 5, 6, or 10 closes the stack and opens a new node bill at the same rate of two always-on nodes per peak pod. Eight nodes for a ceiling of 4 is that rate. Ten nodes for a ceiling of 5 is that rate. Twelve nodes for a ceiling of 6 is that rate. Twenty nodes to restore a ceiling of 10 is that rate, and it is one zone's whole [0104](0104-per-zone-node-max.md) max, paid while idle.

[0106](0106-api-single-zone-hostname-floor.md) already refused a fourth hostname because the floor does not use it. [0107](0107-api-hostname-ceiling.md) already refused buying hostnames so a burst can avoid sharing. The middles are a smaller copy of that refused bill. This slice does not pay it. The close that does not open a new bill is the ceiling the floor already has hostnames for.

Why the empty hostnames on a healthy pool are not a new bill. Two zones are still six nodes. The ceiling of 3 uses three of them. Zone `maxSkew` 1 places those pods 2 and 1. Each zone's three hostnames place those as 1 each. The empty ones are the other zone's floor, which [0106](0106-api-single-zone-hostname-floor.md) already pays so one dead zone can still hold 1 and 1 and 1. They are not spare burst capacity.

Why the HPA floor stays 3. The budget keeps 2 pods of the live color during a voluntary eviction. `minReplicas` has to be at least `minAvailable + 1`, which is 3. The ceiling meets that floor and does not go under it. The metrics, the behavior windows, and the blue target stay.

`minDomains` is the wrong lever. Unset, it behaves as 1, so one hostname still schedules. A floor of 2 treats the missing hostname as an empty domain and leaves the second pod Pending. Kind and minikube have one node. Required hostname anti-affinity does the same. Required zone anti-affinity would refuse the one Ready zone. Neither is set.

This slice does not reopen presence/CSP, Hikari/replica pool sizing, bundle zip, cosign, CDN edge, secrets rotation, VerifyFieldBounds, ClientAddress, rate-limit bucket sizes, the machine/admin HMAC, the nonce store, the download JWT `jti`, the WAF ACL, Redis AUTH, Postgres JDBC SSL, API listener TLS, the HPA metrics, the API PDB `minAvailable`, the API hostname/zone topology spread, the metrics-server zone `DoNotSchedule` item, the node-pool pin or taint, Cluster Autoscaler replica count, required hostname anti-affinity, leader election, the disruption budget, aws-node, vpc-cni, or the kube-proxy reassert hook beyond this cross-link. The [0104](0104-per-zone-node-max.md) max stays 20. The [0106](0106-api-single-zone-hostname-floor.md) floor stays 3. The [0101](0101-cluster-autoscaler-leader-scale.md) flags stay `--balance-similar-node-groups=true`, `--expander=least-waste`, and `--skip-nodes-with-system-pods=false`. Do not set `minDomains`. Required hostname anti-affinity is not the follow-up. Required zone anti-affinity is not the follow-up. Catalog stays 221. No storefront. No DirectX 12 / Vulkan / Solana. No Rui sprites.

## Decision

**`maxReplicas` is 3. That equals `minReplicas` and equals `min_size` 3, so one Ready zone holds the ceiling at one pod per hostname under hostname `maxSkew` 1. The HPA cannot scale above the floor. `min_size` and the create-time `desired_size` stay 3. `max_size` stays 20. The eight-node, ten-node, twelve-node, and twenty-node bills are refused. Hostname and zone spread stay `DoNotSchedule` with `maxSkew` 1 and `minDomains` unset. Kind and minikube do not apply the HPA and do not plan the pool. No live AWS apply.**

1. **Ceiling.** `maxReplicas` is 3. `hpa-max-replicas=3` and `hostname-ceiling-pods=3` and `single-zone-hostname-ceiling=3` name it. `refused-ceiling-nodes-per-pod=2` names the rate this slice will not pay. The [0107](0107-api-hostname-ceiling.md) ceiling of 6 is refused. A value of 4, 5, 7, or 10 is refused. There is no variable that overrides the ceiling.
2. **Why 3 does not stack when only one zone is Ready.** Eligible hostnames are the Ready nodes in that zone. Hostname `maxSkew` 1 refuses a second pod while any of those three is empty. Three pods run out at that step. The zone item, with `minDomains` unset, still admits every ceiling pod into the one zone. Similar groups keep the same `min_size`, the same `instance_types`, the same pool label, and the same `NoSchedule` taint. `--balance-similar-node-groups=true` stays.
3. **Why the pool floor does not rise.** `min_size_per_zone` stays 3. `desired_size_per_zone` stays 3. Four per zone is eight nodes so a ceiling of 4 could sit 1 each, and the fourth hostname is one the floor does not use. Five per zone is ten nodes. Six per zone is twelve nodes so the [0107](0107-api-hostname-ceiling.md) ceiling could sit 1 each on one zone. Ten per zone is twenty nodes so the old ceiling of 10 could sit 1 each on one zone. All four bills are two always-on nodes per peak pod. All four are refused. `max_size_per_zone` stays 20. A zone at 20 still cannot grow.
4. **Why the HPA floor stays 3 and the range collapses.** `minReplicas` stays 3. `minAvailable` stays 2. Three is `minAvailable + 1`. The ceiling is that same 3, so the HPA cannot scale up and cannot scale down. CPU stays 70 percent of the `250m` request. Memory stays an absolute `800Mi`. Scale up stays 2 pods per 60 seconds. Scale down stays a 300 second wait and 1 pod per 60 seconds. The blue target stays. Those windows do not move a count that is already both bounds.
5. **What this slice does not change.** The hostname item stays `DoNotSchedule`, `maxSkew` 1, Honor, `minDomains` unset. The zone item stays the same shape. Required hostname anti-affinity is not set. Required zone anti-affinity is not set. Do not set minDomains. One hostname still schedules both local blue replicas. The second pod is not left Pending by the hostname item. Terraform still ignores `desired_size` after create (`ignore_changes`). The [0106](0106-api-single-zone-hostname-floor.md) keeper caveat is unchanged: a group whose desired size is still 2, or still 1, needs that bump before `min_size` 3 will apply. The HPA, the PDB, and the autoscaler file stay out of the kustomization. Blue stays `replicas: 2`. Green stays `replicas: 0`. No live AWS apply.
6. **Kind and minikube.** `enable_node_pool=false` does not plan the groups. `kubectl apply -k deploy/k8s` does not apply `hpa.yaml` or `pdb.yaml`. One eligible hostname still runs both local blue replicas once the node carries `computerpets/node-pool=api` and one `topology.kubernetes.io/zone` value. Do not set `minDomains`. Do not add required hostname anti-affinity. Do not taint a kind or minikube node.
7. **Verify without a cluster.** `check-api-single-zone-hostname-ceiling.sh` fails when `maxReplicas` is not 3 (including the [0107](0107-api-hostname-ceiling.md) ceiling of 6, and 4, 5, 7, and 10), when that ceiling stacks on one Ready zone, when the ceiling is not the floor, when `min_size` is not 3 (including 4, 5, 6, and 10), when `desired_size` is not 3, when `max_size` is not 20, when the always-on count is not six, when the zone gate is not two, when hostname `maxSkew` is not 1, when hostname or zone spread leaves `DoNotSchedule` or sets `minDomains`, when required anti-affinity appears, when `minReplicas` is not 3, when `minAvailable` is not 2, when `kustomization.yaml` lists `hpa.yaml`, or when local replica counts move. It also fails closed when a refused bill would be the live floor: eight, ten, twelve, or twenty nodes. `check-api-single-zone-hostname-ceiling.test.sh` proves those regressions. No `kubectl apply` and no `terraform apply`.

## Consequences

- The billable floor stays six on-demand workers (default `t3.medium`), three per zone. This slice does not add nodes. Peak replica count drops from 6 to 3. The three pods the healthy pool could have run are the pods this ceiling will not run, because they are the second layer when only one zone is Ready. That is the packing cost. The node cost of keeping a ceiling above 3 is two extra workers per extra pod, paid while the API sits at the floor.
- On one Ready zone the live color's 3 pods are 1 each. A crash of one node takes one of them. Two remain, which is the count the PDB keeps during a voluntary drain.
- On a healthy pool the same ceiling is 2 and 1. Hostname `maxSkew` 1 still places those as 1 each. Three hostnames stay empty because they are the other zone's floor.
- The HPA object stays. Applying it, once metrics answer, holds the live color at 3. It does not scale. Changing `maxReplicas` does nothing until a keeper applies `hpa.yaml`. This slice does not apply. Terraform still ignores `desired_size` after create.
- Kind and minikube do not plan the pool and do not apply the HPA. One hostname still schedules. The second local replica is not left Pending. Do not set `minDomains`. Required hostname anti-affinity is not set.
- Setting the ceiling back to 6, to 4, to 5, to 7, or to 10, or raising `min_size` to 4, 5, 6, or 10 to keep a higher ceiling, or setting `minDomains`, fails `check-api-single-zone-hostname-ceiling.sh`.
- Catalog stays 221. No Rui sprites. `_*.py` stay untracked.
- **Next gap:** a zone already at max 20 cannot grow. Restoring a ceiling above 3 reopens the refused bills (eight nodes for a ceiling of 4, ten for 5, twelve for 6, twenty for 10) and is not the follow-up. Do not set `minDomains`. Required hostname anti-affinity is not the follow-up. Required zone anti-affinity is not the follow-up. The Pending standby still does not hold the lease. One labeled zone still schedules both Cluster Autoscaler pods when two hostnames exist. The kube-proxy managed addon schema still rejects `tolerations`, so coverage between applies depends on the [0097](0097-kube-proxy-toleration-hook.md) hook and on the flag being true. One labeled zone still schedules both metrics-server pods when two hostnames exist. One hostname still schedules every API pod of the live color.
