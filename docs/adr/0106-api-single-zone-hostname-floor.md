# 0106. Three hostnames in the one Ready API zone

- **Status:** Accepted (the healthy-pool ceiling is [0107](0107-api-hostname-ceiling.md); the floor of 3 stays)
- **Date:** 2026-09-23
- **Code:** `deploy/terraform/modules/node_pool/main.tf`; `deploy/k8s/hpa.yaml`; `deploy/k8s/pdb.yaml`; `deploy/k8s/check-api-single-zone-hostname-floor.sh`

## Context

[0105](0105-api-hostname-floor.md) left this gap: one unhealthy zone still places the HPA floor as 2 and 1 on that zone's two hostnames. Inventory on `main` tip `874317e5c`:

| Surface | What it did | What it did not do |
|---------|-------------|--------------------|
| `min_size_per_zone` | A local, `2`, on every similar group. Create-time `desired_size` is `2`, ignored after create | Two hostnames in the one zone that is still Ready. Not three |
| Zone gate | `length(var.subnets) >= 2`. One group per subnet | Not a third availability zone. Two subnets still plan |
| `deploy/k8s/hpa.yaml` | `minReplicas: 3`, `maxReplicas: 10`, target `computerpets-blue`. Not in the kustomization | Not a hostname count. The floor is pods |
| `deploy/k8s/pdb.yaml` | `minAvailable: 2` on the live color. Not in the kustomization | A budget of 2 with only 2 Ready pods allows zero voluntary evictions. The HPA floor stays `minAvailable + 1` |
| Hostname item ([0100](0100-api-hostname-hard-spread.md)) | `kubernetes.io/hostname`, `maxSkew: 1`, `DoNotSchedule`, Honor policies, `minDomains` unset, no required anti-affinity | On two hostnames, 3 pods is 2 and 1. That skew is legal. The third pod binds. Nothing stays Pending, so the leader does not add a node |
| Zone item ([0095](0095-api-zone-hard-spread.md)) | `topology.kubernetes.io/zone`, `maxSkew: 1`, `DoNotSchedule`, `minDomains` unset | One eligible zone still schedules. All three floor pods enter it. The zone item does not invent a second zone |
| `max_size_per_zone` | `20` ([0104](0104-per-zone-node-max.md)) | A ceiling. Not this floor |
| Kind and minikube | Blue `replicas: 2` on one hostname. `enable_node_pool=false` does not plan the groups | Must still schedule. `minDomains` and required hostname anti-affinity would leave the second local replica Pending |

Why the HPA floor stays 3. The budget keeps 2 pods of the live color during a voluntary eviction. `minReplicas` has to be at least `minAvailable + 1`, which is 3. House math does not lower the HPA floor to make two hostnames enough.

Why two hostnames still stack. Hostname `DoNotSchedule` with `maxSkew` 1 allows a difference of one. A second pod on a hostname is illegal only while some other eligible hostname is still empty: the skew would be 2. After both hostnames hold one pod, the third pod's skew is 1, so it binds. The placement is 2 and 1. Cluster Autoscaler sees no Pending pod.

Why three hostnames do not stack. The same rule, on three hostnames, refuses a second pod on any hostname while one hostname is still empty. Three pods run out before every hostname has one. The only feasible placement is 1 and 1 and 1. That is `min_size_per_zone` equal to `minReplicas`, measured against one Ready zone, not against two healthy zones.

Why not a third availability zone with the floor of 2. One unhealthy zone out of three would leave four hostnames, and the floor would not stack. The named case is only one zone's nodes Ready. That zone would still have two hostnames, and the floor would still be 2 and 1. The zone gate stays `>= 2`. A plan with exactly two subnets stays valid. Do not raise the gate to 3 to keep the old floor.

Why not 4. Three hostnames already force 1 and 1 and 1. A fourth hostname per zone bills two more nodes and does not change the floor placement. It is refused.

`minDomains` is the wrong lever. Unset, it behaves as 1, so one hostname still schedules. A floor of 2 treats the missing hostname as an empty domain and leaves the second pod Pending. Kind and minikube have one node. Required hostname anti-affinity does the same. Required zone anti-affinity would refuse the one Ready zone. Neither is set.

The [0104](0104-per-zone-node-max.md) max stays 20. The [0101](0101-cluster-autoscaler-leader-scale.md) flags stay `--balance-similar-node-groups=true`, `--expander=least-waste`, and `--skip-nodes-with-system-pods=false`. Do not set `minDomains`. Required hostname anti-affinity is not the follow-up. Required zone anti-affinity is not the follow-up. Catalog stays 221. No storefront. No DirectX 12 / Vulkan / Solana. No Rui sprites.

## Decision

**Each API zone's managed node group `min_size` is 3, and the create-time `desired_size` is 3. One Ready zone is three schedulable API hostnames, so the HPA floor of 3 is 1 and 1 and 1 under hostname `maxSkew` 1. The zone gate stays two. HPA `minReplicas` stays 3 because the PDB `minAvailable` is 2. Hostname and zone spread stay `DoNotSchedule` with `maxSkew` 1 and `minDomains` unset. Kind and minikube do not plan the pool. No live AWS apply.**

The `maxReplicas` of 10 in point 4 is the part [0107](0107-api-hostname-ceiling.md) replaces. `minReplicas` stays 3.

1. **Floor.** `min_size_per_zone` is the local `3`. `desired_size_per_zone` is the local `3`, because EKS requires `min_size <= desired_size <= max_size` at create. Both are copied into `scaling_config`. A second literal is refused. `single-zone-hostname-floor=3` names one Ready zone times this floor. `hostname-floor-nodes=6` names two healthy zones times this floor. `hpa-min-replicas=3` and `pdb-min-available=2` name the numbers the floor is measured against. The [0105](0105-api-hostname-floor.md) local of 2 is refused. A value other than 3, including 0, 1, and 4, is refused. There is no `variable "min_size_per_zone"`.
2. **Why 3 is enough when only one zone is Ready.** Eligible hostnames are the Ready nodes in that zone. Hostname `maxSkew` 1 refuses a second pod on a hostname while any eligible hostname is empty. Three pods on three hostnames cannot take that step. The zone item, with `minDomains` unset, still admits every floor pod into the one zone. Similar groups keep the same `min_size`, the same `instance_types`, the same pool label, and the same `NoSchedule` taint. `--balance-similar-node-groups=true` stays.
3. **Why the zone count stays two.** The zone gate stays `length(var.subnets) >= 2`. A third AZ with `min_size` 2 does not give the one Ready zone a third hostname. This slice does not require three subnets.
4. **Why the HPA floor stays 3.** `minReplicas` stays 3. `maxReplicas` stays 10. `minAvailable` stays 2. Three is `minAvailable + 1`, so one pod can be evicted and two stay. The metrics, the behavior windows, and the blue target stay.
5. **What this slice does not change.** The hostname item stays `DoNotSchedule`, `maxSkew` 1, Honor, `minDomains` unset. The zone item stays the same shape. Required hostname anti-affinity is not set. Required zone anti-affinity is not set. Do not set minDomains. One hostname still schedules both local blue replicas. The second pod is not left Pending by the hostname item. `max_size_per_zone` stays 20. Terraform still ignores `desired_size` after create. The HPA, the PDB, and the autoscaler file stay out of the kustomization. Blue stays `replicas: 2`. Green stays `replicas: 0`. No live AWS apply.
6. **Kind and minikube.** `enable_node_pool=false` does not plan the groups. `kubectl apply -k deploy/k8s` does not apply `hpa.yaml` or `pdb.yaml`. One eligible hostname still runs both local blue replicas once the node carries `computerpets/node-pool=api` and one `topology.kubernetes.io/zone` value. Do not set `minDomains`. Do not add required hostname anti-affinity. Do not taint a kind or minikube node.
7. **Verify without a cluster.** `check-api-single-zone-hostname-floor.sh` fails when `min_size_per_zone` is not 3 (including the [0105](0105-api-hostname-floor.md) floor of 2), when `desired_size` is not 3 or falls outside min and max, when `scaling_config` hardcodes either size, when a variable can override the local, when the zone gate is not two, when one Ready zone's hostnames are below `minReplicas`, when hostname `maxSkew` is not 1, when that spread would still allow two floor pods on one hostname, when `minReplicas` is not 3, when `minAvailable` is not 2, when `minReplicas` is not at least `minAvailable + 1`, when hostname or zone spread leaves `DoNotSchedule` or sets `minDomains`, when required anti-affinity appears, when `kustomization.yaml` lists `hpa.yaml`, or when local replica counts move. `check-api-single-zone-hostname-floor.test.sh` proves those regressions. No `kubectl apply` and no `terraform apply`.

## Consequences

- Two healthy zones start at three on-demand workers each (default `t3.medium`). That is six nodes, the billable floor. The leader can grow a zone up to 20 and cannot shrink it below 3. Scale-down does not restore the two-hostname pool.
- On that floor, one Ready zone holds the live color's 3 pods as 1 and 1 and 1. A crash of one node takes one of them. Two remain, which is the same count the PDB keeps during a voluntary drain. While the group is replacing that node, the zone is briefly two hostnames again, and hostname `maxSkew` 1 allows 2 and 1 until the third node is Ready. This slice does not add a fourth hostname for that window.
- Changing the Terraform local does nothing to a live group until a keeper applies it. This slice does not apply. `ignore_changes` on `desired_size` means an already-created group whose desired size is still 2 (the [0105](0105-api-hostname-floor.md) floor, after that keeper bump) or still 1 (if that bump never landed) will not move that size to 3 on the apply. EKS rejects `min_size` 3 while desired is still below 3. A keeper raises desired to at least 3 in the same change window, or creates the group from this file.
- Above the floor, `maxReplicas` 10 on six healthy hostnames is still inside `maxSkew` 1 (2, 2, 2, 2, 1, and 1). Those pods share nodes. On one Ready zone the same ceiling is 4, 3, and 3 on three hostnames, which is also inside `maxSkew` 1. Nothing stays Pending, so the leader does not add hostnames for the ceiling.
- Kind and minikube do not plan the pool. One hostname still schedules. The second local replica is not left Pending. Do not set `minDomains`. Required hostname anti-affinity is not set.
- Setting the local back to 2, raising it past 3, dropping `desired_size` under the min, lowering the HPA floor to 2, raising the zone gate to 3 to keep the old floor, or setting `minDomains` fails `check-api-single-zone-hostname-floor.sh`.
- Catalog stays 221. No Rui sprites. `_*.py` stay untracked.
