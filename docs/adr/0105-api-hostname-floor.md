# 0105. Two hostnames per API zone for the HPA floor

- **Status:** Accepted (the single-zone floor is [0106](0106-api-single-zone-hostname-floor.md); the per-zone floor of 2 is superseded in part)
- **Date:** 2026-09-23
- **Code:** `deploy/terraform/modules/node_pool/main.tf`; `deploy/k8s/hpa.yaml`; `deploy/k8s/pdb.yaml`; `deploy/k8s/check-api-hostname-floor.sh`

## Context

[0104](0104-per-zone-node-max.md) left this gap: on two hostnames the HPA floor is 2 and 1, so two pods of one color can still share a node. Inventory on `main` tip `ce7186e80`:

| Surface | What it did | What it did not do |
|---------|-------------|--------------------|
| `deploy/k8s/hpa.yaml` | `minReplicas: 3`, `maxReplicas: 10`, target `computerpets-blue`. Not in the kustomization | Not a hostname count. The floor is pods |
| `deploy/k8s/pdb.yaml` | `minAvailable: 2` on the live color. Not in the kustomization. Apply only after the floor is running | A budget of 2 with only 2 Ready pods allows zero voluntary evictions. The HPA floor is the spare |
| Hostname item ([0100](0100-api-hostname-hard-spread.md)) | `kubernetes.io/hostname`, `maxSkew: 1`, `DoNotSchedule`, Honor policies, `minDomains` unset, no required anti-affinity | On two hostnames, 3 pods is 2 and 1. That skew is legal. The third pod binds. Nothing stays Pending |
| Zone item ([0095](0095-api-zone-hard-spread.md)) | `topology.kubernetes.io/zone`, `maxSkew: 1`, `DoNotSchedule`, `minDomains` unset. At least two zones | The heavy zone at the floor holds `ceil(3/2)` = 2 pods. Those two share a node when the zone has one hostname |
| `min_size_per_zone` | A local, `1`, on every similar group. Two zones are two workers | Not three schedulable API hostnames. Cluster Autoscaler will not add a node for a pod that already bound |
| Create-time `desired_size` | `1`, ignored after create ([0083](0083-cluster-autoscaler.md)) | Must be at least `min_size` or EKS rejects the group. A later apply does not write it back |
| `max_size_per_zone` | `20` ([0104](0104-per-zone-node-max.md)) | A ceiling. Not this floor |
| Kind and minikube | Blue `replicas: 2` on one hostname. `enable_node_pool=false` does not plan the groups | Must still schedule. `minDomains` and required hostname anti-affinity would leave the second local replica Pending |

Why the HPA floor stays 3. The budget keeps 2 pods of the live color during a voluntary eviction. `minReplicas` has to be at least `minAvailable + 1`, which is 3. A floor of 2 would match a two-hostname pool and would also satisfy hostname `maxSkew` 1 as 1 and 1, and zone spread of 2 pods across 2 zones is 1 and 1. It would also make the budget block every drain. That is the shape `pdb.yaml` already refuses on a laptop. House math does not lower the HPA floor.

Why the pool floor rises. Hostname `DoNotSchedule` with `maxSkew` 1 allows a difference of one. Three pods on two hostnames is 2 and 1, which is inside that skew, so the scheduler binds all three and Cluster Autoscaler sees no Pending pod. The third hostname is never created. The fix is to have the hostnames before the pods land. Similar groups stay one per zone, at least two, with the same floor. The heavy zone under zone `maxSkew` 1 holds 2 of the 3 floor pods. That zone needs 2 hostnames so those two are 1 and 1. `min_size_per_zone` 2 does that. Two healthy zones are 4 hostnames, which is at least the HPA min. A floor of 1 is the old 2-and-1 pool and is refused. A floor of 3 would bill six nodes so one dead zone could still hold the floor 1 and 1 and 1. That is the remaining gap, not this slice.

`minDomains` is the wrong lever. Unset, it behaves as 1, so one hostname still schedules. A floor of 2 treats the missing hostname as an empty domain and leaves the second pod Pending. Kind and minikube have one node. Required hostname anti-affinity does the same. Neither is set.

This slice does not reopen presence/CSP, Hikari/replica pool sizing, bundle zip, cosign, CDN edge, secrets rotation, VerifyFieldBounds, ClientAddress, rate-limit bucket sizes, the machine/admin HMAC, the nonce store, the download JWT `jti`, the WAF ACL, Redis AUTH, Postgres JDBC SSL, API listener TLS, the HPA metrics and replica range, the API PDB `minAvailable`, the API hostname/zone topology spread, the metrics-server zone `DoNotSchedule` item, the node-pool pin or taint, Cluster Autoscaler replica count, required hostname anti-affinity, leader election, the disruption budget, aws-node, vpc-cni, or the kube-proxy reassert hook beyond this cross-link. The [0104](0104-per-zone-node-max.md) max stays 20. The [0101](0101-cluster-autoscaler-leader-scale.md) flags stay `--balance-similar-node-groups=true`, `--expander=least-waste`, and `--skip-nodes-with-system-pods=false`. Do not set `minDomains`. Required hostname anti-affinity is not the follow-up. Required zone anti-affinity is not the follow-up. Catalog stays 221. No storefront. No DirectX 12 / Vulkan / Solana. No Rui sprites.

## Decision

**Each API zone's managed node group `min_size` is 2, and the create-time `desired_size` is 2. Two healthy zones are four schedulable API hostnames, so the HPA floor of 3 does not land 2 and 1. HPA `minReplicas` stays 3 because the PDB `minAvailable` is 2. Hostname and zone spread stay `DoNotSchedule` with `maxSkew` 1 and `minDomains` unset. Kind and minikube do not plan the pool. No live AWS apply.**

The floor of 2 in that decision is the part [0106](0106-api-single-zone-hostname-floor.md) replaces. One Ready zone at that floor is still 2 and 1.

1. **Floor.** `min_size_per_zone` is the local `2`. `desired_size_per_zone` is the local `2`, because EKS requires `min_size <= desired_size <= max_size` at create. Both are copied into `scaling_config`. A second literal is refused. `hostname-floor-nodes=4` names two zones times this floor. `hpa-min-replicas=3` and `pdb-min-available=2` name the numbers the floor is measured against. The old local of 1 is refused. A value other than 2, including 0 and 3, is refused. There is no `variable "min_size_per_zone"`.
2. **Why 2 is enough when both zones are up.** The zone gate stays `length(var.subnets) >= 2`. Zone `maxSkew` 1 places the 3 floor pods as 2 and 1. The heavy zone's 2 hostnames place those pods as 1 and 1 under hostname `maxSkew` 1. The light zone places its one pod on one of its two hostnames. No hostname holds two pods of the live color. Similar groups keep the same `min_size`, the same `instance_types`, the same pool label, and the same `NoSchedule` taint. `--balance-similar-node-groups=true` stays.
3. **Why the HPA floor stays 3.** `minReplicas` stays 3. `maxReplicas` stays 10. `minAvailable` stays 2. Three is `minAvailable + 1`, so one pod can be evicted and two stay. A floor of 2 is refused even though two hostnames could hold it without stacking. The metrics, the behavior windows, and the blue target stay.
4. **What this slice does not change.** The hostname item stays `DoNotSchedule`, `maxSkew` 1, Honor, `minDomains` unset. The zone item stays the same shape. Required hostname anti-affinity is not set. Required zone anti-affinity is not set. Do not set minDomains. One hostname still schedules both local blue replicas. The second pod is not left Pending by the hostname item. `max_size_per_zone` stays 20. Terraform still ignores `desired_size` after create. The HPA, the PDB, and the autoscaler file stay out of the kustomization. Blue stays `replicas: 2`. Green stays `replicas: 0`. No live AWS apply.
5. **Kind and minikube.** `enable_node_pool=false` does not plan the groups. `kubectl apply -k deploy/k8s` does not apply `hpa.yaml` or `pdb.yaml`. One eligible hostname still runs both local blue replicas once the node carries `computerpets/node-pool=api` and one `topology.kubernetes.io/zone` value. Do not set `minDomains`. Do not add required hostname anti-affinity. Do not taint a kind or minikube node.
6. **Verify without a cluster.** `check-api-hostname-floor.sh` fails when `min_size_per_zone` is not 2 (including the old 1), when `desired_size` is not 2 or falls outside min and max, when `scaling_config` hardcodes either size, when a variable can override the local, when the zone gate is not two, when the hostname product of that gate and the per-zone floor is below `minReplicas`, when the per-zone floor is below the heavy-zone pod count, when `minReplicas` is not 3, when `minAvailable` is not 2, when `minReplicas` is not at least `minAvailable + 1`, when hostname or zone spread leaves `DoNotSchedule` or sets `minDomains`, when required anti-affinity appears, when `kustomization.yaml` lists `hpa.yaml`, or when local replica counts move. `check-api-hostname-floor.test.sh` proves those regressions. No `kubectl apply` and no `terraform apply`.

## Consequences

- Two healthy zones start at two on-demand workers each (default `t3.medium`). That is the billable floor. The leader can grow a zone up to 20 and cannot shrink it below 2. Scale-down does not restore the old two-hostname pool.
- On that floor the live color's 3 pods do not share a node. A crash of one node takes one of them. Two remain, which is the same count the PDB keeps during a voluntary drain.
- Changing the Terraform local does nothing to a live group until a keeper applies it. This slice does not apply. `ignore_changes` on `desired_size` means an already-created group whose desired size is still 1 will not move that size to 2 on the apply. EKS rejects `min_size` 2 while desired is still 1. A keeper raises desired to at least 2 in the same change window, or creates the group from this file.
- One unhealthy zone still places the HPA floor as 2 and 1 on that zone's two hostnames. Hard hostname spread allows it. The floor of 2 is `ceil(minReplicas / minZones)` for two healthy zones, not a third hostname in the surviving zone.
- Above the floor, `maxReplicas` 10 on four hostnames is still inside `maxSkew` 1 (3, 3, 2, and 2). Those pods share nodes. Nothing stays Pending, so the leader does not add hostnames for the ceiling.
- Kind and minikube do not plan the pool. One hostname still schedules. The second local replica is not left Pending. Do not set `minDomains`. Required hostname anti-affinity is not set.
- Setting the local back to 1, raising it past 2, dropping `desired_size` under the min, lowering the HPA floor to 2, or setting `minDomains` fails `check-api-hostname-floor.sh`.
- Catalog stays 221. No Rui sprites. `_*.py` stay untracked.
