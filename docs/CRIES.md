Better tape is always welcome. We hunt tape on the internet, cut 2–4 seconds, drop it in [`cries-in/`](../cries-in/), and sit it into `desktop/renderer/sounds/` and `web/public/sounds/`. Each real tape keeps a `SOURCE-*.txt` next to the drop. The mailbox is [`cries-in/GROK-CHANNEL.md`](../cries-in/GROK-CHANNEL.md). Every guest has a cry now. Four still play habitat recordings instead of their own voice — **Sol** (iguana), **Shift** (chameleon), **Spike** (horned lizard), and **Lid** (box turtle) — until open-licensed tape of the species exists. No inventing beeps.

No music beds. No English narrator. If it sounds like a phone UI, it does not ship.
